Mighty Motion

Lead Programmer · FTC Team #8721 Mighty Mechanics · 2023–2026

A custom Java motion-control library and robot codebase for FIRST Tech Challenge that replaced the open-source libraries our team had relied on. It ran our robot through the 2026 season, when we won the NYC Championship and the FTC Control Award and qualified for the World Championship in Houston.

  • Built our own localization, motion profiling, holonomic pure pursuit, PID pose control and mecanum inverse kinematics, plus tuning OpModes for each controller.
  • Fused goBILDA Pinpoint odometry with Limelight 3A AprilTag poses through parallel 1D Kalman filters, and isolated a Limelight firmware defect that returned NaN rotations for near-head-on poses.
  • Built AutoAim: a turret angle solver plus a quadratic distance-to-RPM regression for the shooter, so aligning a shot became a single action.
  • Cut autonomous overshoot by regressing braking distance against velocity and decelerating early.

ProfiledPathGenerator

Solo project · FTC autonomous path planning · 2025

Turns a handful of field waypoints into a smooth path the robot can drive in autonomous. Instead of hand-tuning arcs, it fits a piecewise parametric cubic spline through every waypoint by solving a single linear system, then samples it into dense points that a path-following controller can track.

  • Builds the 8n × 8n constraint system directly: waypoint continuity (assembled with a Kronecker product), C¹ and C² continuity at every interior knot, and zero velocity at the start and end.
  • Solves for every spline coefficient at once with EJML, then discretizes each segment into 50 points.
  • Plots the result on the 144-inch field with JFreeChart, so a route can be checked before it runs on the robot.
  • Later ported into Mighty Motion, where I connected it to a geometric path-following controller and live dashboard graphing.

Research Experience

My research focuses on medical AI and computer vision, with hands-on work in deep learning systems for chest X-ray analysis and prior engineering research in materials reuse.

June 2025 - Present

Research Intern | Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research, Northwell Health

Division of Health Artificial Intelligence — Mentors: Dr. Theo Zanos and Dr. Nabil Ettehadi

Project: Multi-model deep learning ensemble for 14-label thoracic pathology classification on NIH ChestX-ray14

  • Built a nine-model PyTorch ensemble on Databricks for 14-label thoracic pathology classification across 112K NIH ChestX-ray14 images.
  • Designed a hybrid CNN–Vision Transformer architecture with residual squeeze-and-excitation stem blocks, mixed-precision training, and a cosine learning-rate schedule with warmup.
  • Built a dual-scale U-Net with Performer attention and EfficientNet fusion; diagnosed a branch-dominance failure suppressing the decoder’s contribution to AUROC and proposed an auxiliary-loss correction.
  • Stacked the base models with scikit-learn and XGBoost meta-learners trained on exported embeddings; resolved solver convergence failures through pipeline-level feature scaling.
  • Integrating RAD-DINO, a chest X-ray–pretrained ViT, as a tenth base model and replacing per-class weighted BCE with Asymmetric Loss.
